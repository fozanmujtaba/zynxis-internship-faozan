Hi,

We're building Kesho, a savings app for informal-sector workers in Kenya —
market traders, boda riders, that sort of thing. These are people who earn
daily in cash and have basically no relationship with a bank. They save
through chamas (informal savings groups) and mostly on paper.

What we want to build is an app where a group can run their chama digitally.
Members contribute weekly, the app tracks who has paid and who hasn't, and
when it's your turn you get the payout. Right now this is all done by one
person with a notebook and it goes wrong constantly — disputes about who paid,
treasurers disappearing with the pot, that kind of thing.

Must haves:
- Works on cheap Android phones, and works when the network drops. Our users
  are not on 5G.
- M-Pesa for money in and out. That's non-negotiable, it's how everyone pays.
- Group admins can see contribution history and chase people who are behind.
- Some kind of trust/reputation thing so members can see who reliably pays.
- English and Swahili.

We'd also love an AI thing that predicts whether someone is likely to default
on their contribution, but honestly that might be phase two, we're not sure.

We have about four months before our seed round and we need something real in
users' hands before then. Budget is tight. There are two of us who can code,
both mostly frontend, so we'd need help on the backend side.

One thing we're nervous about: the regulatory side. We're not a bank and we
don't want to accidentally become one. We've heard the CBK has rules about
holding customer funds but we don't really know what applies to us.

Can you put together a plan for what this looks like and what it'd take?

Thanks,
Amara
